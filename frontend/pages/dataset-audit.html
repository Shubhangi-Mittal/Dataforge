📝 Dataset Audit Report

Upload one dataset and get a combined audit covering schema validation, data quality, EDA, anomaly scans, and next steps.

What this tool does: Dataset Audit Report is the unified command center for evaluating a new dataset before it reaches dashboards, models, or production pipelines.
  • Runs schema validation and shows structural issues
  • Checks quality and completeness across columns
  • Builds an EDA summary with insights and correlations
  • Scans numeric columns for anomaly signals
  • Saves the final report to audit history for later review

Best for small to medium datasets. Very large files may be rejected or sampled during the heavier analysis steps.
