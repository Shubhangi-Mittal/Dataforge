🧳 Lakehouse Organizer

Simulated S3 lakehouse — ingest files, auto-partition, convert to Parquet, and catalog metadata.

What this tool does: Simulate S3 lakehouse management — ingest files, auto-partition by date and source, convert to Parquet format, and catalog metadata.
  • Upload files to ingest into the lakehouse with automatic partitioning
  • Browse the file catalog and view partition structure
  • Track storage stats, format breakdown, and conversion rates
  • Files are organized by date/source partitions for efficient querying
